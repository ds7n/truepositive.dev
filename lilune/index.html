Lilune

A gentle migraine diary for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

One tap to start tracking, even from your Watch or the Lock Screen. Soft colors that are easy on light-sensitive eyes. A calendar and a list of every migraine, and a doctor report when you need one.

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your diary stays on your device. Every feature is free.

Lilune is a diary, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice.
