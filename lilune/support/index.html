Lilune Support

Lilune is a gentle migraine diary for iPhone and Apple Watch. Questions or problems: lilune@truepositive.dev. Please include your device and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I start tracking? Tap Track Migraine on Home. You can also use the Action button, a Control Center or Lock Screen control, the widget, Siri ("Track migraine in Lilune"), or the Apple Watch app.

It started earlier than when I tapped. How do I fix the time? Right after starting, choose 30 min ago, 1 h ago, 2 h ago, Woke up with it, or pick a time. Later, tap the "since..." line on the tracking screen, or open the day (Calendar) or the migraine (Episodes) and tap Details.

I left the tracking screen. Is it still running? Yes. Tracking only stops when you tap Stop Tracking (in the app, on the widget, or on the Watch).

Why don't I see air pressure for a migraine? Lilune reads your iPhone's barometer at start and stop. It needs Motion permission (iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness > Lilune). The reading is taken on the iPhone.

How do I change the pressure unit? Settings > Air pressure: hPa, mbar, inHg, mmHg, or kPa.

Is there an iPad version? Not yet. Lilune is made for iPhone and Apple Watch. An iPad can run the iPhone app, but it keeps its own separate diary.

Sleep or Cycle turned itself off. Lilune could not read that data from Apple Health. Allow it in the Health app > Sharing > Apps > Lilune, then switch it on again.

How do I share with my doctor? The report button (top right on Calendar or Episodes) makes a PDF for the last 30 or 90 days. Settings > Export all data gives CSV files with everything.

How do I delete everything? Settings > Delete all data.

Does Lilune cost anything? No. Every feature is free.

Privacy

See the privacy policy: https://truepositive.dev/lilune/privacy. In short: no account, no server, no ads, no analytics.

Lilune is a diary, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice.
