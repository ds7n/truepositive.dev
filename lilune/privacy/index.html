Lilune Privacy Policy

Effective: November 21, 2026

Lilune is a migraine diary for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, made by True Positive LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into Lilune. The short version: we never receive it. Lilune has no account, no server, no ads, and no analytics.

What Lilune stores

Everything you record: when each migraine started and ended, how bad it was, symptoms, how your day went, where it hurt, possible triggers, notes, your medications and the doses you log, your answers to the optional evening check-in, and your settings.

Lilune also stores the air pressure your iPhone's barometer measures when you start and stop tracking. It does not use your location.

Where it is stored

  • On your device only. Your diary is stored in Lilune's private, encrypted storage on your iPhone (and a small status copy on your Apple Watch, so it can show whether you are tracking). Lilune does not sync your diary to iCloud or anywhere else.
  • Device backups. Like all app data, your diary is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup if you use one, so it comes back on a new iPhone. Apple handles those backups (see https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); turning on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud makes them end-to-end encrypted.

We do not operate any server, and Lilune sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

Apple Health

If you turn on Sleep or Cycle in Settings, Lilune asks for permission to read your sleep and menstrual flow data from Apple Health. Lilune reads it only to show it next to your migraines (last night's sleep, cycle day, period days). It is read when shown and is never copied into Lilune's storage, never sent anywhere by Lilune, and never shared except in a doctor report you choose to share. Lilune does not write anything to Apple Health. You can withdraw permission at any time in the Health app.

Health data from Apple Health is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and is never disclosed to third parties.

Notifications and Motion

Lilune schedules reminders on your device (for example "Did it help?" after a dose, or the evening check-in). Motion permission lets it read the barometer. Neither sends data anywhere.

Sharing you choose

When you make a doctor report (PDF) or export your data (CSV), Lilune creates the file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice. If Cycle is on, the doctor report includes how many migraines started near a period start, worked out from Apple Health. Lilune deletes these files as soon as you close the report or export screen.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no data brokers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No sale or sharing of any information.

Your control

  • Edit or delete any entry in the app.
  • Settings > Delete all data removes every migraine, medication, dose, and check-in.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from the device.

Children

Lilune is not directed to children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

Lilune is a diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
